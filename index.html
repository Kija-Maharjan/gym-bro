🤝
Partner System
  • One goes, one rests — natural load balance
  • 100 kg coaches pull-up form on 71 kg's sets
  • 71 kg times holds and counts reps for 100 kg
  • No ego lifting — finish your reps, don't rush
📋
Programming Logic
  • Same movement, scaled reps/variant
  • 5 days on, 2 days off (Sat/Sun rest)
  • Deload week every 4th week (–30% volume)
  • Skill days = always train together
🥩
Nutrition Reminder
  • 71 kg: slight surplus if gaining, ~2.2g protein/kg
  • 100 kg: maintenance or slight cut, 2.4g/kg
  • Both: eat within 1hr post-session
  • Sleep 7–9h — most gains happen here
🔀
When to Diverge
  • 100 kg adds rings/weighted vest when bodyweight feels easy
  • 71 kg reduces sets (not reps) if form breaks
  • If 71 kg gets 10+ pull-ups: join 100 kg's track
  • Adjust every 4 weeks based on progress
